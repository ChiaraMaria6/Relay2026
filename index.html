Teams

We organize teams to have similar finish times, but also adhering to those requests where runners want to run together. We have runners of all paces.

Vision

There is a communal tent for hanging out in your camp chair / cooking / board games / socializing ... with surrounding sleeping tents. This allows everyone to be social when they want and get some privacy when needed. No glamping as this journey is part of the great experience.

Volunteers

We need leaders for fun tasks like bringing games, camp lights, decorations.

Packing

Basically ya need to bring a backpack with your clothes, shoes, etc. The more stuff ya bring, the more stuff ya carry.

Save Space

We lighten the load by organizing who will bring what to avoid repetition (ie just need one med kit) and helping to minimize trash.

Tents

You can bring your own tent. However, we have a large 20-person tent plus several smaller tents, so you do not need one.

Cots

We highly recommend renting a cot from the race for $20 each cot.

No Trash

Every person needs to bring a container for cold liquids and another one for hot liquids (your hot chocolate moment).

Water

We have access to a large Gatorade cooler, so instead of bringing tons of plastic water bottles, each member uses their water bottle to refill your drinks. The race provides free water too.

Food

There are plenty of food trucks. However, we ask runners to bring a special dish that make the experience fun and delicious, like homemade dips, brownies, fresh cut fruits, coquito, or mulled apple cider (which is great when it is cold). If we get cooks, a grill, and food (burgers, breakfast) … we can be amazed with the most delicious meal that feels great after trail running and camping!

Campsite Tips

Campsite Selection and Setup

Once at the event, teams choose their own campsite. Each team gets a 20’ x 20’ area. Choose a quieter location near the edge of camp when possible.

Communal Tents

Plan for 1–2 communal 10’ x 10’ tents for snacks, games, and shared space.

Coolers

Race provides water. We will bring one large Gatorade cooler with water so we minimize plastic bottles.

Chairs

Everyone should bring their own camping chair.

Cots

Everyone should bring a cot or rent one at the race for $20.
